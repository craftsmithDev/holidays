# Switzerland: sources and verification

State: 15 November 2026. Rules: [rules.yaml](rules.yaml). The comparison with external data for 2024–2030 (generated by `node scripts/compare.mjs CH`) is at the [end of this file](#comparison-with-external-data).

## Sources and method

1. **Reference dataset.** The Swiss data of the craftsmith visibility plugin (`src/support/holidays/ch.php`), ported date for date. `tests/golden/ch-2026-2030.json` holds its dates for all 26 cantons, 2026–2030; the golden tests in both runtimes compare against it. Every deliberate difference is listed in `tests/golden/ch-deviations.json` and explained below.
2. **Type of each day.** The SECO list of cantonal holidays equal to Sunday under the Labour Act, ArG Art. 20a para. 1, state 12 December 2024 ([PDF](https://www.seco.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/x5LtHPJdNis5/Anhang-Feiertage-Schweiz-Auszug-ArG-ArGV1-5-SECO-SB-2024-DE.pdf)). The table was read cell by cell from the PDF coordinates, including the exception pages for AG, AI, AR, FR, NE and SO. 1 August is federal (BV Art. 110 para. 3).
3. **Cantonal law** where SECO points to an exception: Neuchâtel RSN 941.02 Art. 3; Glarus Ruhetagsgesetz GS IX B/21/1 Art. 2.
4. **Cross-check, not a source**: OpenHolidays API and the npm package date-holidays.

The Federal Office of Justice list of cantonal holidays (`bj.admin.ch/…/kant-feiertage.pdf`, the list under the European Convention on the Calculation of Time Limits) returned HTTP 404 on 15 November 2026. It is not cited.

## Deliberate differences from the reference dataset

Every entry here is in `tests/golden/ch-deviations.json`; the golden tests fail on any other difference.

### Saint Stephen in AI and AR

The SECO exception pages: Appenzell Ausserrhoden does not observe Saint Stephen when Christmas falls on a Monday or Friday; Appenzell Innerrhoden observes it only when it does not create three rest days in a row, which is the same two cases. The reference had the day every year.

Now: `public` with `adjust: skip` when 26 December is a Tuesday or Saturday. Removed: 2026-12-26 and 2028-12-26 in AI and AR. OpenHolidays agrees for AI (no 26 December in 2026 and 2028) but keeps AR; date-holidays keeps both (as observance in AI). The SECO list is the official source; both external sets are wrong for AR.

### Labour Day afternoon in SO

SECO lists Labour Day afternoon (“Tag der Arbeit nachmittags”) for the whole canton of Solothurn, including the district Bucheggberg. The reference had no Labour Day in SO.

Now: `public`, `startTime: "12:00"`. Added: 1 May 2026–2030 in SO. date-holidays has it (as a whole day), OpenHolidays does not.

### All Saints in SG

SECO lists All Saints for St. Gallen. The reference did not.

Now: `public`. Added: 1 November 2026–2030 in SG. Both OpenHolidays and date-holidays agree.

### Days equal to Sunday in part of a canton

SECO equates some days with Sunday only in parts of four cantons. The reference listed some of them (AG Berchtold’s Day, Easter Monday, Whit Monday, Saint Stephen; all the confessional days of FR) and left out others. They are now all listed the same way: an `observance` at canton level, with a note naming the districts or municipalities (decision D6).

| Canton | Holiday | Where it is equal to Sunday (SECO) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| AG | Corpus Christi | districts Baden (except Bergdietikon), Bremgarten, Laufenburg, Muri, Zurzach; six municipalities of Rheinfelden | added |
| AG | Assumption | Bremgarten, Laufenburg, Muri; six municipalities of Rheinfelden | added |
| AG | All Saints | Bremgarten, Laufenburg, Muri, Zurzach, Rheinfelden | added |
| AG | Immaculate Conception | Laufenburg, Muri; six municipalities of Rheinfelden | added |
| SO | Corpus Christi, Assumption, All Saints | whole canton except the district Bucheggberg | added |
| NE | Corpus Christi | municipality Le Landeron | added |
| FR | Immaculate Conception | predominantly Catholic municipalities | added |

OpenHolidays and date-holidays both have FR Immaculate Conception; date-holidays has AG and SO Corpus Christi, Assumption and All Saints (as public, which overstates it for the parts where they are not equal to Sunday).

## Corrections without effect on 2026–2030

### Neuchâtel substitute days

RSN 941.02 Art. 3: 2 January and 26 December are rest days equal to Sunday only when 1 January or 25 December is a Sunday. Now: a `substitute` on the NE observances of New Year and Christmas (`ch.new-year` on 2023-01-02, `ch.christmas` on 2033-12-26). The reference had 2 January every year and no 26 December. 2 January stays as a customary `observance` in NE, so the golden dates for 2026–2030 do not change.

## Classification (type) changes against the reference

The reference had no types. Each canton’s entry is now `public` if SECO lists the day for that canton, otherwise `observance`. Examples: Berchtold’s Day is public in BE, TG and VD and an observance in ZH; Whit Monday is an observance in LU and TI; Jura Independence Day (23 June) is an observance because Jura already equates eight other days.

### Whit Monday in Glarus

The Glarus Ruhetagsgesetz (Art. 2) lists Whit Monday among the public rest days and says they are equal to Sunday under the Labour Act. The SECO list does not count Whit Monday for Glarus. Glarus would otherwise equate nine days, while ArG Art. 20a allows eight. The data follows SECO (`observance`) and cites both sources in the note. Open question for a lawyer or the Glarus labour inspectorate.

## Not modelled yet

- Municipal holidays: Sechseläuten and Knabenschiessen (city of Zurich), local patron saints, the district rules of AG and SO, the confessional split of FR. They need the municipal level (format.md, “Reserved for later”).
- Official Romansh names. The `rm` names are machine-written and not yet checked against a Romansh source (decision D12, docs/coverage.md).
- Bank holidays (see the table above).
