# Liechtenstein: verification

State: 15 November 2026. Data: `data/li/`. Report: [report-li.md](report-li.md) (generated by `node scripts/compare.mjs LI`, years 2024–2030).

## Sources

The visibility plugin has no Liechtenstein data, so the country was researched from official sources:

1. **Labour Act** (Arbeitsgesetz, LR 822.10), Art. 18 para. 2, version in force since 1 January 2019, read on gesetze.li ([PDF](https://nextmobile.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/1967006000?version=14)). It lists the holidays equal to Sunday: New Year, Epiphany, Easter Monday, 1 May, Ascension, Whit Monday, Corpus Christi, Assumption, Nativity of Mary, All Saints, Immaculate Conception, Christmas, Saint Stephen. These 13 days are `public`.
2. **Calendar of the national administration** (LLV, Amt für Personal und Organisation), “Feiertage und dienstfreie Tage” for [2024](https://www.llv.li/serviceportal2/amtsstellen/amt-fuer-personal-und-organisation/kalender-der-llv/kalender-2024.pdf) and [2025](https://www.llv.li/serviceportal2/amtsstellen/amt-fuer-personal-und-organisation/kalender-der-llv/kalender-2025.pdf). The 2026 calendar returned HTTP 404. Besides the 13 days it lists Candlemas (2 February) and Saint Joseph (19 March) as holidays, and Berchtold’s Day, Shrove Tuesday, Good Friday, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve as whole days off for its staff. These seven are `observance` (decision D13). The bridge days after Ascension and Corpus Christi (“Feiertagsbrücke”) are internal to the administration and not listed.

The LLV calendar names 15 August “Staatsfeiertag”; the Labour Act calls it Maria Himmelfahrt. The id is `li.national-day`, the names carry both.

## Differences with OpenHolidays

All 13 `public` days agree in every year 2024–2030. OpenHolidays has none of the seven `observance` days (Berchtold’s Day, Candlemas, Shrove Tuesday, Saint Joseph, Good Friday, Christmas Eve, New Year’s Eve). Ours are sourced from the LLV calendar and typed `observance`, so a default query (type `public`) returns the same days as OpenHolidays.

## Differences with date-holidays

| Difference | Who is right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Theirs only: “Feiertagsbrücke” (public), the Fridays after Ascension and Corpus Christi | ours (left out) | Bridge days of the national administration only; the LLV calendar lists them for its own staff. Not a holiday for anyone else. |
| Theirs only: Easter Sunday, Whit Sunday (observance) | both | Sundays are not listed (decision D7). |
| Theirs only: Mother’s Day | ours (left out) | Not a holiday. |

date-holidays has the seven LLV days as well; there are no other date differences.

## Open points

- The LLV calendar is the employer calendar of the administration. If a law or the bank association defines bank holidays for Liechtenstein, some `observance` days may become `bank`.
- The LLV calendar 2026 was not reachable; the sources cite 2024 and 2025.
