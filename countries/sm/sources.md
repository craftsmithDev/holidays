# San Marino: sources and verification

State: 15 November 2026. Rules: [rules.yaml](rules.yaml). The comparison with external data for 2024–2030 (generated by `node scripts/compare.mjs SM`) is at the [end of this file](#comparison-with-external-data).

## Sources

1. **Legge 18 dicembre 1990 n. 152, “Calendario delle Festività”**, in force since 1 January 1991, read in the law archive of the Consiglio Grande e Generale ([PDF](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17021104.html)).
   - Art. 1: “Sono festive” Sundays, 1 January, 6 January, Corpus Domini (the Thursday after Trinity Sunday), 15 August, 1 November, 2 November, 8 December, 25 December. “Sono previste” as religious and national holidays: 5 February (Sant’Agata) and 3 September (San Marino).
   - Art. 2: “Sono festive” Easter Monday and 26 December. “Sono inoltre previste” as civil and national holidays: 25 March (Arengo), 1 April and 1 October (Ingresso dei Capitani Reggenti), 1 May, 28 July (Caduta del Fascismo).
   - Art. 3: no longer holidays: Saint Joseph, Ascension, Saints Peter and Paul, the afternoon of the last day of carnival, 14 and 16 August, 24 and 31 December.
   - Art. 5: labour law and the rules on civil acts follow this list.
2. **Calendario degli uffici 2026**, Segreteria di Stato per gli Affari Interni (20 November 2025). It marks as “Vacanza” every weekday holiday of the law in 2026: 1 and 6 January, 5 February, 25 March, 1 April, 6 April, 1 May, 4 June, 28 July, 3 September, 1 October, 2 November, 8 December, 25 December. 15 August, 1 November and 26 December fall on a weekend in 2026. 24 and 31 December are working days.
3. **Decreto Delegato 26 febbraio 2026 n. 30, Calendario scolastico 2026–2027**, and Decreto Delegato 20 febbraio 2025 n. 25 (2025–2026). Their list of festività has the same dates, with Corpus Domini on 4 June 2026 and 27 May 2027.

## Type rule

`public` = a holiday of Legge 152/1990 Art. 1 or 2. The law separates days that “sono festive” from days that “sono previste” as national holidays. The office calendar 2026 closes the State’s offices on both kinds, and Art. 5 adapts the labour law to the whole list, so the data does not split them. No `observance` days.

## Regions

The 9 castelli (SM-01 to SM-09) are listed so that ISO codes are accepted (decision D-sm-1). No holiday differs between them; the patron-saint days of the castelli are municipal and not listed.

## Second pass: 2025, 2026, 2027

Easter Monday: 21 April 2025, 6 April 2026, 29 March 2027. Corpus Domini: 19 June 2025, 4 June 2026, 27 May 2027. Every other day is fixed. The 2026 dates were checked one by one against the office calendar 2026, the dates of January to July 2027 against the school calendar 2026–2027, and the 2025 dates against the law and the computus. No difference found.

## Open points

- **Name of 28 July.** A bill to rename the day “Anniversario della Caduta del Fascismo e Festa della Libertà” passed committee on 27 June 2013 (Commissione I, report and text in the Consiglio archive). The enacted law was not found. OpenHolidays and date-holidays use the longer name; the school calendar 2026–2027 uses “Anniversario della Caduta del Fascismo”. The date is the same either way.
- **Labour law.** The day-off effect for private employers was not read in the labour law itself; it rests on Art. 5 of Legge 152/1990 and the office calendar.
