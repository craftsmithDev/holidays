# Malta: verification

State: 15 November 2026. Data: `data/mt/`. Report: [report-mt.md](report-mt.md) (generated by `node scripts/compare.mjs MT`, years 2024–2030).

## Sources

The National Holidays and Other Public Holidays Act, Cap. 252 (Act XXX of 1975 as amended up to Act IV of 2021), read in English and Maltese on legislation.mt ([EN PDF](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/252/eng/pdf), [MT PDF](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/252/mlt/pdf)).

- Art. 2: five National Holidays: 31 March (Jum il-Ħelsien), 7 June (Sette Giugno), 8 September (Jum il-Vittorja), 21 September (Jum l-Indipendenza), 13 December (Jum ir-Repubblika). Art. 3 makes them public holidays.
- Art. 5(1) and the Schedule: nine further public holidays: 1 January, 10 February, 19 March, Good Friday, 1 May, 29 June, 15 August, 8 December, 25 December.
- The list was last substituted by Act VIII of 1989, so it is the same for 2000–2050.

All 14 days are `public`. Malta has no regional holidays; `regions` is empty.

Names: Maltese from the Maltese text. The English text uses the Maltese names for the National Holidays; the English names in the data (Freedom Day, Victory Day, Independence Day, Republic Day) are the usual English names, Sette Giugno is kept as it is.

## Weekend rule

The task brief assumed that the 2005 rule (no replacement for a holiday on a weekend) lasted until 2023. Art. 6 ends it on 1 January 2021:

- Art. 6(b), Act II of 2005: from 1 January 2005, a holiday on a Saturday or Sunday does not count as a public holiday for an extra day of vacation leave.
- Art. 6(c), Act IV of 2021 (sidenote in the English text: IV.2020.2; Maltese text and title: IV.2021.2): from 1 January 2021, such a holiday gives one day of vacation leave on top of the yearly entitlement under the Organisation of Working Time Regulations (S.L. 452.87, reg. 8).

Neither rule moves a holiday or names a substitute date. Since 2021 the employee gets an extra leave day to take when agreed, not a fixed day off. The data therefore has no `substitute` and no `adjust` for Malta, in every year (decision D-pt-2). An expectation case checks that Sette Giugno 2026 (a Sunday) has no substitute.

## Differences with OpenHolidays and date-holidays

None, 2024–2030. date-holidays also matches for every year 2000–2050 (checked with a script outside the report).

## Second pass

2025, 2026 and 2027 were checked again by hand against the Act: the 13 fixed days plus Good Friday (18 April 2025, 3 April 2026, 26 March 2027). All matched; nothing had to be fixed.

## Open points

- Art. 5(2) lets the Prime Minister add or cancel a holiday by notice in the Government Gazette. No such notice was searched for; one-off days would be added as `list` dates.
- The extra leave day since 2021 is an entitlement, not a date. It cannot be expressed in this format and is only described here and in `country.yaml`.
- Bank holidays (Central Bank of Malta, TARGET2) were not researched.
