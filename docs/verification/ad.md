# Andorra: verification

State: 15 November 2026. Data: `data/ad/`. Report: [report-ad.md](report-ad.md) (generated by `node scripts/compare.mjs AD`, years 2024–2030).

## Sources

1. **Llei 31/2018, del 6 de desembre, de relacions laborals**, Art. 62 (BOPA núm. 3, 10 January 2019, [PDF](https://www.consellgeneral.ad/fitxers/documents/lleis-2018/llei-31-2018-de-relacions-laborals)). It gives employees the right to the holidays of the labour calendar and leaves the list to that calendar. It names no dates.
2. **The yearly Govern decree on the labour calendar.** Read for:
   - 2024: Decret 487/2023, del 25-10-2023, BOPA núm. 132, 2 November 2023. The BOPA pages were read from a copy hosted by addwill.ad ([PDF](https://addwill.ad/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Andorra-Decret-aprovacio-calendari-laboral-2024.pdf)).
   - 2025: Decret 409/2024, del 23-10-2024, BOPA núm. 119, 30 October 2024 ([PDF on govern.ad](https://www.govern.ad/documents/1898932/2637160/GD_2024_10_24_14_36_16.pdf/fd9c2b79-c57d-c7af-09f0-8727af22d8a5?t=1731066478995)).
   - 2026 and 2027: the Govern’s publication of the approved decree with the full list of days ([2026](https://www.govern.ad/ca/w/s-aprova-el-calendari-laboral-per-al-2026), 8 October 2025; [2027](https://www.govern.ad/ca/w/s-aprova-el-calendari-laboral-per-al-2027), 21 October 2026). The BOPA text of these two decrees was not read.

The decree lists 14 days, every year the same set: Cap d’Any, Reis, Carnaval, Dia de la Constitució (14 March), Divendres Sant, Dilluns de Pasqua, Festa del treball, Dilluns de Pentecosta, L’Assumpció, Nostra Senyora de Meritxell (8 September), Tots Sants, Immaculada Concepció, Nadal, Sant Esteve. They are paid, not recuperable and compulsory for construction, industry, offices, banks, hairdressers, transport and services not directly related to tourism (Art. 1). In sectors directly related to tourism the same number of days applies, but they may be moved by agreement, except 1 January, 14 March, 8 September and 25 December (Art. 2). All 14 are `public`; the ten movable ones carry a note (D-ad-1).

Carnival is set by the decree: Tuesday 20 February 2024, Monday 3 March 2025, Monday 16 February 2026, Monday 8 February 2027. It is a date list.

Parish holidays: Art. 3 of the decree adds up to four days fixed by each comú and published in the BOPA. They differ by parish and are municipal, so they are out of scope. The parishes are listed as regions (ISO 3166-2:AD, D-ad-1); every national day is nationwide.

Coverage: 2024–2027. Before 2024 and from 2028 the library returns nothing for Andorra, because the law has no list of its own and older decrees were not read.

## Differences with OpenHolidays

| Difference | Who is right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Carnival 2024: ours Tuesday 20 February, theirs Monday 12 February | ours, open | Decret 487/2023 Art. 1 lists “20 Carnaval (dimarts)”. In 2025–2027 the decree takes the Monday before Ash Wednesday, which for 2024 would have been 12 February. The decree was read from a copy of the BOPA page; confirm against the BOPA original. |
| Theirs only: all days 2028–2030 | open by design | No decree exists yet for these years (D-ad-1). |

All other days 2024–2027 agree.
