# Austria: sources and verification

State: 15 November 2026. Rules: [rules.yaml](rules.yaml). The comparison with external data for 2024–2030 (generated by `node scripts/compare.mjs AT`) is at the [end of this file](#comparison-with-external-data). A second run over 2016–2023 was read as well; its differences are covered below.

## Sources and method

1. **Arbeitsruhegesetz** (ARG, BGBl. Nr. 144/1983), § 7 para. 2, current version on RIS. It lists the 13 Feiertage of every employee: New Year, Epiphany, Easter Monday, 1 May (Staatsfeiertag), Ascension, Whit Monday, Corpus Christi, Assumption, 26 October (Nationalfeiertag), All Saints, Immaculate Conception, Christmas, Saint Stephen. These are `public`. The Feiertagsruhegesetz 1957 § 1 has the same list and is cited as well.
2. **Good Friday.** The ARG version of 1 January 2019 (§ 7 para. 3) and of 1 January 2000 make Good Friday a Feiertag only for members of the Protestant churches AB and HB, the Old Catholic and the Methodist church. BGBl. I Nr. 22/2019 (promulgated 21 March 2019) repealed para. 3 and added § 7a, the “persönlicher Feiertag”: once a year an employee may set one day of annual leave unilaterally. Since 2019 Good Friday is a Feiertag for nobody.
3. **Landespatrone and Landesfeiertage.** The federal Schulzeitgesetz 1985 § 2 makes the feast of the Land patron school-free in every Land. The dates come from the Land laws on RIS: Burgenland LBedG 2020 § 11 and the agricultural school law § 15 (11 November), Carinthia K-SchG § 74 (19 March and 10 October), Lower Austria NÖ LV Art. 7 (Landespatron Leopold, Landesfeiertag 15 November), Upper Austria Oö. LFSchG § 15 (4 May; until 31 August 2004: 15 November), Salzburg LwSchG 2018 § 15 (24 September), Styria SchZAG § 2 (19 March), Tyrol SchOG § 110 (19 March), Vorarlberg Pflichtschulzeitgesetz § 3 (19 March), Vienna Schulgesetz § 56 (15 November).
4. **Cross-check, not a source**: OpenHolidays API and date-holidays.

## Classification

`public` is a Feiertag under ARG § 7 para. 2.

`observance`:

| Holiday | Where | Why not `public` |
|---|---|---|
| Good Friday, until 2018 | all | Feiertag only for members of four churches (ARG § 7 para. 3, old version); D4. |
| Good Friday, from 2019 | all | No Feiertag; employees may use § 7a. Kept as an observance so the change of 2019 stays visible; the note explains § 7a. |
| Saint Joseph (19 March) | Carinthia, Styria, Tyrol, Vorarlberg | Land patron; school-free under the Land school laws, a working day under the ARG. |
| Saint Florian (4 May) | Upper Austria, from 2005 | Land patron since 2004. The school law names 4 May from the school year 2004/05. |
| Saint Rupert (24 September) | Salzburg | Land patron, as above. |
| Plebiscite Day (10 October) | Carinthia | Landesfeiertag, school-free (K-SchG § 74), a working day under the ARG. |
| Saint Martin (11 November) | Burgenland | Land patron; a Feiertag for staff of the Land (LBedG 2020 § 11). |
| Saint Leopold (15 November) | Lower Austria, Vienna; Upper Austria until 2003 | Land patron, as above. |
| Christmas Eve, New Year’s Eve | all | No Feiertag under the ARG. Often off or a half day by collective agreement; schools are closed. |

Easter Sunday and Whit Sunday are not listed (D7). All Souls’ Day (2 November) is school-free in every Land but a working day for everyone else; it is not listed.

## Second pass: dates 2025, 2026, 2027

The `public` days were checked again against the official holiday calendar of the City of Vienna: the [list for 2026–2028](https://www.wien.gv.at/amtshelfer/feiertage/) and the [ICS file](https://www.wien.gv.at/spezial/daten/ics/feiertage.ics), which has the movable days back to 2018. All 13 dates of 2025, 2026 and 2027 match (Easter Monday 2025-04-21, 2026-04-06, 2027-03-29; Ascension 05-29, 05-14, 05-06; Whit Monday 06-09, 05-25, 05-17; Corpus Christi 06-19, 06-04, 05-27). The federal Ferienkalender 2026/2027 on oesterreich.gv.at gives the same dates for October 2026 to August 2027.

The `observance` days are fixed dates (19 March, 4 May, 24 September, 10 October, 11 November, 15 November, 24 and 31 December) and were checked against the Land laws named above. Good Friday is Easter Sunday minus two days: 2025-04-18, 2026-04-03, 2027-03-26.

No date had to be corrected.

## Open points

- **Upper Austria 2004.** The school law has 15 November until 31 August 2004 and 4 May from 1 September 2004, so neither day was school-free in calendar year 2004. Whether the Land administration closed on 4 May or 15 November 2004 was not checked; the data has no patron day for Upper Austria in 2004.
- **Land administration.** Only Burgenland (LBedG 2020 § 11) was checked for the staff rules. In the other Länder the patron day is sourced as school-free only.
- **Earlier versions of the school laws.** RIS has consolidated versions for Burgenland, Upper Austria, Styria, Tyrol, Vorarlberg and Salzburg back to 2001 or earlier, all with the same dates. For Carinthia and Vienna the consolidated text of 2001 does not contain the provision, so the dates before about 2003 rest on the current law.
- **Yom Kippur** and other days that collective agreements grant to members of certain religions are not listed.
